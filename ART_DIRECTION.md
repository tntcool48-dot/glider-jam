# Glider Jam — Art Direction

**Setting: "Golden Pillars"**: a valley of sheer sandstone pillars (think Zhangjiajie), ~40 minutes before sunset.
The level's route is unchanged: a 7 km corridor that slaloms between rock towers from the start to the finish gate.

## Mood
Vast scale and speed. A low, warm sun rakes across layered sandstone, throwing long shadows between the towers.
Blue haze and mist pool in the valley floor; the cloud base sits just above the flight ceiling, so gliders skim and
punch through cloud bottoms. Calm landscape, frantic race.

## Time of day and light
- Sun elevation ~10–12°, warm (≈3500 K at the horizon), placed ahead-left of the route so riders fly into
  rim light, god rays between pillars and lens flares on turns.
- Physically based sky, HDRP volumetric clouds (cumulus field, base ~300 m), volumetric fog with denser valley mist.
- Physical exposure, ACES tonemapping, warm-highlights / cool-shadows grading, restrained bloom, subtle vignette,
  motion blur at speed, depth of field only in menus.

## Palette
| Role | Color |
|---|---|
| Sun highlights | amber gold `#F2B66B` |
| Sandstone | terracotta `#A8653A` |
| Pine forest | deep green `#1F3A26` |
| Shadow / haze | slate blue `#4A6385` |
| Clouds | warm cream `#F4E6D0` |
| Player 1 | signal red `#FF2E4D` |
| Player 2 | electric cyan `#1EC8FF` |

Player colors are saturated and cool/warm opposites so each glider, trail, marker and HUD reads instantly against
green forest, sandstone and blue haze.

## Glider
Modern composite racing sailplane (15 m span, slender pod-and-boom fuselage, T-tail, winglets). Gloss white
clear-coated body, player-colored race livery with race number, tinted canopy with pilot, animated control
surfaces, wingtip nav lights and a fin strobe, wingtip vapor trails at speed.

## References
1. **Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024**: atmosphere, cloud scale, terrain readability from the air.
2. **Horizon Forbidden West**: golden-hour light, vegetation density, misty valleys.
3. **Avatar: Frontiers of Pandora** / Zhangjiajie photography: pillar silhouettes and layered depth.
4. **Riders Republic** (wingsuit): speed feel, camera and FOV at speed.

## Assets
- Terrain textures: Poly Haven CC0 (`cliff_side`, `rocky_terrain_02`, `aerial_rocks_02`, `mossy_rock`).
- Everything else (glider, trees, terrain meshes, surrounding mountains, VFX textures) is generated by the
  editor/runtime scripts in this repo.
